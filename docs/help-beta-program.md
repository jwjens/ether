# Beta Program & Feedback

## What it is

A page in Settings with three tabs:

- **About the Program** — what the beta partner program is and what it asks of you.
- **Apply** — a short form about your station, sent to the Ether team.
- **Send Feedback** — tell the Ether team about a bug, ask for a feature, or say thanks.

Sending either form needs an internet connection. Nothing you send changes your account, your plan or your stations.

## Apply to the beta program

1. Open **☰ Menu → Settings**, pick **System** on the left, and find **Beta Program & Feedback**.
2. Click the **Apply** tab.
3. Fill in at least **Station name** and **Email**. The rest helps us, but is optional.
4. Click **Submit application**.
5. When the Ether server has it, you'll see **✓ Application received**, and the tab then reads **✓ Applied**.

## Send feedback

1. Open the same page and click **Send Feedback**.
2. Pick what kind it is: **Bug**, **Feature request**, **Kudos** or **Other**.
3. Write it in **Tell us**. For a bug, say what happened and what you expected.
4. Add your email if you'd like a reply (optional).
5. Click **Send feedback**. **✓ Thanks — we got it** means the Ether server has it, and the box clears.

## If it doesn't send

Ether only says "received" or "we got it" once the Ether server confirms it saved what you sent. If it didn't, a red
message says why — for example no internet connection, the server being down, too many sent from your network in the
last 15 minutes, or a field that needs fixing (like an email address that doesn't look right). **What you typed stays
in the form**, so you can try again.

If you'd rather not wait, click **Email it instead**. Ether opens your email app with what you typed already filled in;
press send there. (Nothing is emailed until you press send in your email app.)

A development build of Ether doesn't send to the Ether server at all; it says so in the same red message.

# Up Next — what plays after the song on air

## What it is
Up Next is the list of what your station plays next, straight from the Program Log, in the log's order. The first
entries are the songs already cued on the standby decks; after them come the rows still waiting.

## When to use it
- To see what is coming in the next few minutes.
- To take something out before it airs (the **X**).

## Taking a song out (the X)
1. Find the song in Up Next.
2. Click its **X**.

**What happens:**
- **The row comes out of the Program Log as well,** not just off the list. It does not come back on the next refresh,
  and it does not air.
- **If the song was already cued on a deck,** the deck is emptied and cued with the next song in the log.
- **The song on air cannot be removed here.** Use the board's **OFF** for the song on air.
- **A row that has already aired, or was marked missed, is the record of what happened.** The X never touches it.

The Program Log shows the gap the X left. Fill Day (or the next Generate) can fill it.

## Good to know
- Ether keeps a record of every song that goes to air: its log row, its category and the computer that played it.
  If anything ever airs twice, or a row that aired is changed later, the **Health Monitor's Alerts** list says so.
- Up Next follows the log. To change what plays later in the day, edit the Program Log.

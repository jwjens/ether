# Phone Desk

## What it is

The **Phone Desk** records a caller from an input on this computer (the phone hybrid or interface plugged in), lets you
trim the clip, and sends it to **Deck A, B or C** or to the **cart wall**. Recording is off air: you hear it in your
headphones only.

## When to use it

Take a call, keep the good part, and play it back on air when you are ready.

## Record and send

1. Press **PHONE** in the bottom bar.
2. Pick the **input** the phone line comes in on. The list is this computer's audio inputs.
3. Press record, take the call, stop.
4. Trim the clip (set the in and out points, or open the full editor).
5. Choose where it goes and send:
   - **A / B / C** — the clip loads onto that deck, ready to play.
   - **CART** — the clip goes onto the **first empty cart** on this station's cart wall, named *Phone Clip* and the time.
     The line under the button says which cart (for example *Sent to Cart 5*). If every cart already has audio, it says
     so and changes nothing — clear a cart, then send again.

Every clip is copied into your catalogue folder, like any other audio, so it travels with your station.

## Not in this version

- **Softphone / SIP.** The **SIP / WEBRTC URL** box is saved but Ether has no phone (SIP) connection of its own yet.
  Plug the phone hybrid or interface into an input and record from that.

# Voice Tracker

## What it is

**Voice Tracker** records your talk breaks ahead of time. Pick an hour, pick the seam between two songs, record, and add
the take to that talk break. It records from the **voice-tracking microphone** you pick in Settings.

## Pick your microphone (once)

1. Open **Settings → Audio Devices**.
2. Under **Voice-tracking microphone**, click the mic you record voice tracks with. It shows **ACTIVE**.
3. That choice is saved on this computer. Voice Tracker uses it in the main window and in its own window.

The on-air mic is set separately, in **Mic Inputs** on the same page.

## Record a talk break

1. Open **☰ → Voice Tracker**.
2. Pick the hour, then click a transition in the **Hour Log** to talk over it.
3. Click **● REC**, speak, and click **■ Stop**.
4. Click **＋ BREAK** to add the take to that talk break, or **Discard take** to throw it away.

## How it behaves

- If the microphone you picked isn't connected, Voice Tracker records from the computer's default microphone and says so
  next to REC: "The voice-tracking mic picked in Settings isn't connected — recording from the default mic."
- If Windows refuses Ether access to the microphone, Voice Tracker says it couldn't open the microphone. It does not
  switch to another one.

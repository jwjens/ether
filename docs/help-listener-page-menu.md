# Listener page menu

## What it is

Your station's public listener page has a narrow strip down its left side with your station logo at the top. Under the
logo is a short menu. It only shows things your station actually has — nothing is shown for something you haven't set
up.

| Item | What it does | When it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Now playing** | Opens the full-screen player: the song on now, what's up next, and what has played while the page was open. | Always, once the station has loaded |
| **Links** | Jumps down to your station's **Links** (for example a Donate button). | Only when your station (or another station on your account) has links |
| **Website** | Opens your station's own website in a new tab. | Only when your station has a website address set |

## Make Links and Website show up

1. In Ether, open **☰ Menu → Settings → Station → Public Listener Page**. (The web dashboard at **app.ether-cast.com**
   → your station → **Station** tab also edits the website address.)
2. To get **Website**: fill in the **website** box among the social links. You can type it with or without `https://`.
3. To get **Links**: under the named links, click **+ Add link** and give it a label (e.g. Donate) and an address
   starting with `http://` or `https://`. Save.
4. Reload the listener page. The new items appear in the left menu.

## Good to know

- Pages with their own special design (for example HalloVeen's funhouse or Magical Forest's radio cabin) don't have
  this strip; they are unchanged.
- There is no schedule or song-request item in this menu. A song-request page you run can still be added as a named
  link, and it will then appear under **Links**.

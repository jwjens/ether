The governing rule: controls are ranked by how often a producer touches them. Transport owns its own bar at the bottom centre and shares it with nothing. Editing tools sit against the editor they act on. Save / export / import / notes / history are demoted to the menubar and a quiet corner. Mockup only — no app code has changed. Built from the app's own tokens.

Rank 3 · a few times a session Save, export, import, notes, history, verify. Menubar and a quiet corner. Never a row of their own.

Waveform rendering — what the canvas must draw

Today

One peak polyline per clip from extractPeaks() at a fixed 2000 points regardless of length or zoom. Mono-summed, unfilled, no RMS. At high zoom it under-samples into straight lines; over a long session it computes detail nobody sees.

Proposed

Per channel — L above, R mirrored below — each pixel column drawing min/max peak as a filled bar at full clip colour, with RMS overdrawn inside at ~55%. That two-layer read is what makes a waveform look professional: transient envelope and perceived energy at once.

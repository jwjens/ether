# Keyboard shortcuts

> **Help corpus template.** Written to the format in `docs/help-sweepers.md`: plain language, step-by-step, the
> section order kept.

## What it is

Ether's shortcuts are **single keys**. You press the key on its own:

- **L** opens the Library.
- **S** opens Schedule / Clocks.
- **G** opens the Play Log.
- **A** turns automation on or off.
- **Shift + ?** shows or hides the shortcut list.
- **Esc** closes the shortcut list and the ☰ menu.
- On a cart wall, **1–8, Q–I, A–K** fire the first 24 carts (see `docs/help-module-carts.md`).

Editors have their own single keys while they are open:
- the Reel Splitter, Voice Tracker, Show+ DAW and phone recorder: Space plays, Delete removes, plus each editor's
  own keys;
- Show+: 1–9 to switch scenes.

## When to use it

Whenever your hands are on the keyboard and you want a screen, automation or a cart without reaching for the mouse.

## Set it up

Nothing to set up. Click anywhere that is **not a text box** first: while you are typing in a box, the keys type.

## Ctrl, Alt and Cmd

**A key held with Ctrl, Alt or Cmd (⌘) is not a shortcut. It does nothing.**

- **Ctrl+S** does not open Clocks.
- **Ctrl+A** does not turn automation on or off.
- **Ctrl+1** does not fire a cart.

So a habit from another program — Ctrl+S to save, Ctrl+A to select all — can never change what is on air.

**Shift is different.** Shift + ? opens the list. Shift makes a bigger or finer step where an editor says so.

The real Ctrl combinations still work where they exist: Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y (undo / redo), Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V (copy /
paste) in the editors.

## Not in this version (by design)

- No key starts, pauses or resumes a deck. The board's ON button is the only way (see `docs/help-deck-on.md`).
- Choosing your own keys: macros can take a single key (Macros), and the same rule applies to them.

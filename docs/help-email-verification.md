# Confirm your email

## What it is

When you sign up for Ether, we email you a link: **Verify your Ether account**. Opening it confirms the email address
is yours.

- **You can start right away.** Your free trial starts the moment you sign up, on a brand-new account of your own.
  You don't have to confirm first to use it.
- **Joining someone else's account waits.** If a station invited you to their account, you can't get into it until
  you confirm: you can't sign in to it, switch to it, see its computers for Remote Link, or set Ether up on a computer
  as part of it. Your invitation is still there; it works as soon as you confirm.

This keeps someone who signs up with your email address from getting into anything that belongs to you.

## The link

1. Open the email **Verify your Ether account** and click **Verify email**.
2. The page says **Your email is verified.** That's it.

- The link lasts **24 hours** and works **once**.
- If it has expired or was already used, the page says the link is invalid or has already been used. Ask for a new
  link (below).
- Resetting your password (**Forgot password**) also confirms your email, because the reset link went to your inbox.

## Get a new link

A new link replaces the old one; the old one stops working. (A button for this on the account page is not built yet;
until it is, a password reset confirms your email the same way.)

## If something goes wrong

- **"Confirm your email address first"** when you open an account you were invited to: you haven't confirmed yet.
  Open the newest link we sent, or reset your password.
- **No email?** Check spam for a message from noreply@ether-technologies.com.

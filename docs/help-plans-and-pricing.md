# Plans and Prices

## What it is

**Plans & Subscription** shows Ether's plans and the plan your account is on. The prices and limits come from Ether's
server each time you open it, so they are always current. Every plan starts with a **15-day free trial with no card**.
Annual billing costs 10 months' price (2 months free).

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Local** | $20/mo or $200/yr, for the whole account | The full desktop app on every computer, your own Icecast stream, updates. No cloud features. |
| **Live 128** | $25/mo per station | One station hosted on Ether's cloud at 128 kbps with 50 listeners, plus every cloud feature (web dashboard, remote, sync, backup, cloud library). |
| **Live 320** | $49/mo per station | One hosted station at 320 kbps with 150 listeners, every cloud feature, and priority support. |
| **Network** | $79/mo for 5 stations | Five hosted stations at 128 kbps sharing 250 listeners, plus every cloud feature. Each extra station is $15/mo and adds 50 listeners to the shared pool. |

Need more listeners on a Live or Network plan? **+50 listeners is $10/mo.**

## How to use it

1. Open **☰ → Plans & Subscription**.
2. Your plan has a highlighted outline and says **Your plan** (or **Yours for life** on a lifetime license).
3. To change plans, click **Choose …** on a card. Your account page opens in your browser; buy the plan there.
4. Come back to Ether. Your new plan applies the next time Ether checks in with the server (within a minute).

**Sign In** and **Enter License Key** at the top of the screen work as before.

## Good to know

- **Lifetime licenses** keep everything they had. They show as **Network (Lifetime)**.
- A license on the old free key shows **Free (old key)** and keeps working as it always has.
- If the plans don't load, this computer can't reach Ether's server right now. Nothing about your license changes;
  open the screen again later.
- Billing never stops audio or playout. See [Billing notices](help-billing-notices.md) for what happens when a trial
  ends or a card is declined.
